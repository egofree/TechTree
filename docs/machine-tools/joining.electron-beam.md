# Electron Beam Welding

> **Node ID**: `machine-tools.joining.electron-beam`
> **Domain**: [Machine Tools](./index.md)
> **Dependencies**: [`energy.electricity`](../energy/electricity.md),
> [`machine-tools.joining`](./joining.md), [`vacuum.pumps`](../vacuum/pumps.md)
> **Enables**: [`vacuum.chambers`](../vacuum/chambers.md)
> **Outputs**: electron_beam_welds, hermetic_seals
> **Timeline**: Years 30-60
> **Critical**: No

Focused electron beam (30-150kV accelerating voltage, 10-100mA beam current) welding in vacuum chamber. Deep narrow penetration (depth-to-width 10:1 to 30:1), minimal heat input and distortion. Zero porosity, zero contamination. Critical for UHV vacuum chamber fabrication (leak rates ≤10⁻¹⁰ mbar·L/s), refractory metals, and aerospace components. Single-pass penetration up to 200mm in steel.
