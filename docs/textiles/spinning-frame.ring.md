# Ring Spinning Frame

> **Timeline**: Years 18-25

Thorpe ring spinning frame (1828): C-shaped traveler rotates around a ring at high speed, simultaneously inserting twist and winding yarn. Continuous motion, simpler mechanism than mule. Dominant spinning system since 1900.
