# Analog Environments

Analog environments are ground-based facilities that simulate aspects of long-duration spaceflight: isolation, confinement, communication delay, limited resources, and harsh external conditions. NASA's HERA facility offers 45-day missions in a 3-story, 18 m³ habitat with simulated Earth-Mars communication delays. The NEEMO programme sends aquanaut crews to the Aquarius Reef Base 19 metres below the sea surface for week-long saturation missions. The SIRIUS programme at IBMP Moscow runs 4-8 month lunar mission simulations in the NEK isolation chamber. Polar analogs (Concordia Antarctica, Devon Island Haughton-Mars) study the psychological and operational effects of extended darkness, extreme cold, and complete isolation.
