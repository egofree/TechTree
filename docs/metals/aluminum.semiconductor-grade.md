> **Outputs**: semiconductor_grade_aluminum, sputtering_targets, high_purity_aluminum
> **Timeline**: Years 40-70
> **Critical**: No

Ultra-high-purity aluminum (5N-6N, 99.999-99.9999%) for semiconductor metallization via three-layer electrolysis (Hoopes process) and zone refining. Produces sputtering targets and evaporation sources for IC interconnect layers, bond pads, and display panel metallization. Critical impurity limits: Fe, Si <3 ppm; U/Th <1 ppb.
