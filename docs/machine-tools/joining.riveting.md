# Riveting

> **Node ID**: `machine-tools.joining.riveting`
> **Domain**: [Machine Tools](./index.md)
> **Dependencies**: None
> **Enables**: [`machine-tools.machining`](../machine-tools/machining.md), [`metals.iron-steel`](../metals/iron-steel.md)
> **Outputs**: riveted_joints
> **Timeline**: Years 10-30
> **Critical**: No

Mechanical joining with rivets — permanent fasteners installed through holes in overlapping plates. Hot rivets contract on cooling, producing preload that makes joints leak-tight and vibration-resistant. Dominated structural steel construction from ~1840 until arc welding replaced riveting after ~1940.
