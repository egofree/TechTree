# Countermeasures

Countermeasures are the daily regimen of exercise, pharmacological intervention, and operational protocol that slows the physiological deconditioning caused by microgravity exposure. The ISS programme allocates 2.5 hours per crew member per day — 1.5 hours of structured exercise plus 1 hour of setup and stowage — split across the ARED resistive exercise device (loads up to 270 kg), the T2 treadmill (bungee-loaded running at 55-80% body weight equivalent), and the CEVIS cycle ergometer (25-350 W). Pharmaceutical countermeasures include weekly alendronate (70 mg) to suppress bone resorption and daily potassium citrate (20 mEq) to buffer metabolic acidosis.
