# Diffusion Bonding

> **Node ID**: `machine-tools.joining.diffusion-bonding`
> **Domain**: [Machine Tools](./index.md)
> **Dependencies**: None
> **Enables**: [`energy.electric-furnaces`](../energy/electric-furnaces.md), [`machine-tools.joining`](../machine-tools/joining.md)
> **Outputs**: diffusion_bonds, hermetic_seals
> **Timeline**: Years 35-65
> **Critical**: No

Solid-state joining at 50-80% melting point temperature under 1-10 MPa pressure for 30-120 minutes. Atomic diffusion across the interface eliminates the joint line — bond indistinguishable from parent metal. Joins dissimilar metals (Ti-to-SS, Cu-to-Al) via thin interlayers without brittle intermetallics. HIP bonding (50-200 MPa isostatic) for complex internal surfaces. Critical for UHV chambers and semiconductor heat exchangers.
