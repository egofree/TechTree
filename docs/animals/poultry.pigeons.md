# Pigeons & Squab

> **Node ID**: `animals.poultry.pigeons`
> **Domain**: [Animals](./index.md)
> **Dependencies**: None
> **Enables**: [`animals.poultry`](../animals/poultry.md)
> **Outputs**: squab_meat, guano, manure
> **Timeline**: Years 0-10+
> **Critical**: No

Domestic pigeons (Columba livia) raised for squab meat (26-30 day old juveniles) and guano fertilizer. Pigeons are self-feeding foragers requiring minimal supplemental feed, and squab reaches market weight (350-500g dressed) in under a month. Pigeon guano is one of the richest natural fertilizers (high NPK).
