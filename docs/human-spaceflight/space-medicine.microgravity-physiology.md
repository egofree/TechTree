# Microgravity Physiology

Microgravity physiology studies the systematic adaptations the human body undergoes in weightlessness. Within hours of arrival, 1.5-2.0 litres of fluid shift from the legs to the head, causing facial edema and raising intracranial pressure. Bone mineral density declines at 1-2% per month at load-bearing skeletal sites. Skeletal muscle atrophies at up to 5% per week without countermeasures. The cardiovascular system loses plasma volume (10-15%) and baroreflex sensitivity, resulting in orthostatic intolerance on return to 1g. Spaceflight-Associated Neuro-ocular Syndrome (SANS) produces optic disc edema in approximately 70% of long-duration crew.
