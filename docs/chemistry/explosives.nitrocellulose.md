# Nitrocellulose & Smokeless Powders

> **Node ID**: `chemistry.explosives.nitrocellulose`
> **Domain**: [Chemistry](./index.md)
> **Dependencies**: [`chemistry.acids`](./acids.md), [`chemistry.explosives`](./explosives.md),
> [`chemistry.solvents`](./solvents.md)
> **Outputs**: nitrocellulose, smokeless_powder, pyroxylin
> **Timeline**: Years 10-20
> **Critical**: No

Nitration of cellulose (cotton linters or wood pulp) with mixed acid to produce nitrocellulose (guncotton) and pyroxylin. Includes manufacture of single-base and double-base smokeless powders via gelatinization, extrusion, and drying. Foundation of the smokeless powder industry and early plastics (celluloid).
