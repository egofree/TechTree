# Vermiculture

> **Node ID**: `agriculture.soil-management.vermiculture`
> **Domain**: [Agriculture](./index.md)
> **Dependencies**: None
> **Enables**: [`agriculture.soil-management`](../agriculture/soil-management.md)
> **Outputs**: vermicompost, worm_castings, worm_tea
> **Timeline**: Years 0-20+
> **Critical**: No

Composting with Eisenia fetida (red wiggler) earthworms to produce vermicompost and worm castings. Worm castings contain 1.5-2.5% N, 1.0-1.5% P, and 1.0-1.5% K — significantly richer than thermophilic compost. Worm populations double every 2-3 months under ideal conditions, and worm tea serves as a potent liquid fertilizer.
