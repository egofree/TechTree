Neutral buoyancy training at the Neutral Buoyancy Laboratory (NBL) is the gold standard for EVA preparation. The NBL pool at NASA's Sonny Carter Training Facility measures 71.6 x 41.1 x 12.3 metres (23.5 million gallons / 62.2 million litres) and contains full-scale ISS mockups including the Destiny lab, Harmony Node 2, truss segments, and the SSRMS robotic arm. A suited astronaut in the pool is weighted and buoyed to net-neutral force, closely approximating the weightless EVA environment. Each training session lasts 6 hours underwater at 1:1 time fidelity to an orbital EVA, supported by a team of 12-15 personnel including 4 safety divers, a test director, and communications operators.
