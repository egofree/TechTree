# Gutta-Percha Processing

> **Node ID**: `polymers.rubber.gutta-percha`
> **Domain**: [Polymers](./index.md)
> **Dependencies**: [`polymers.rubber`](./rubber.md)
> **Enables**: [`marine.infrastructure`](../marine/infrastructure.md), `telecom`,
> [`telecom.submarine-cables`](../telecom/submarine-cables.md)
> **Outputs**: gutta_percha, underwater_insulation, dental_fillings
> **Timeline**: Years 15-30
> **Critical**: No

Trans-1,4-polyisoprene from Palaquium tree latex. Hard, horn-like thermoplastic at room temperature, softens at 60-80°C. Harvested by felling trees and collecting coagulated latex. Processing: wash, masticate, roll into sheets. The ONLY practical underwater electrical insulation until polyethylene (1940s). Used for submarine telegraph cables, dental fillings, tool handles, electrical insulation.
