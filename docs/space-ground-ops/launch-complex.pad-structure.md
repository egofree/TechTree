# Pad Structure & Flame Trench

> **Node ID**: space-ground-ops.launch-complex.pad-structure
> **Domain**: [Space Ground Operations](./index.md)
> **Dependencies**: [`space-ground-ops.launch-complex`](./launch-complex.md)

> **Outputs**: launch_pads
> **Critical**: No

The pad structure is the reinforced concrete hardstand, flame trench, and deflector assembly that supports the launch vehicle and survives the 2,500°C exhaust plume at liftoff. The LC-39A/B flame trench is a 13m-deep × 18m-wide × 137m-long open channel carved below the pad deck, lined with refractory silica brick rated to 1,650°C. The wedge-shaped flame deflector (60° apex angle, 635 tonnes at LC-39A) splits the exhaust plume into two lateral jets directed away from the vehicle. Acoustic deluge suppression injects up to 1,000,000 gallons/min (3,785 m³/min) of water onto the deflector and pad deck at T-6.6 seconds, absorbing 15 dB of the 165 dB unsuppressed acoustic environment and extending refractory life from 1-2 launches to 6-10.

## Process Overview

1. **Foundation**: Drive steel pipe piles to bedrock; pour reinforced concrete pad deck
2. **Flame trench**: Excavate 13m-deep channel; line with 0.6-1.2m refractory concrete walls
3. **Deflector**: Cast 635-tonne wedge deflector; install on rail system for maintenance withdrawal
4. **Deluge system**: Install 88m elevated tank (1.135M L); plumb spray headers at 2.4 MPa
5. **Refractory**: Apply silica brick lining; commission with test fire
