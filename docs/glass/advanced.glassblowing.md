# Glassblowing and Scientific Apparatus

> **Node ID**: `glass.advanced.glassblowing`
> **Domain**: [Glass](./index.md)
> **Dependencies**: [`energy.fuels`](../energy/fuels.md), [`glass.advanced`](./advanced.md)
> **Outputs**: scientific_glassware, glass_apparatus, glass_to_metal_seals
> **Timeline**: Years 10-25
> **Critical**: No

Lampworking and freehand glassblowing for laboratory and technical apparatus. Torch types (Bunsen, gas-oxygen, hydrogen-oxygen). Standard products: round-bottom flasks, condensers, distillation columns, thermometers, vacuum manifolds, ampoules. Glass-to-metal seals for electrical feedthroughs. Essential for ALL chemistry lab work beyond test tubes.
