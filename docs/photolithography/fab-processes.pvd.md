# Physical Vapor Deposition (PVD)

> **Node ID**: `photolithography.fab-processes.pvd`
> **Domain**: [Photolithography](./index.md)
> **Dependencies**: None
> **Enables**: [`metals.specialty-semiconductor`](../metals/specialty-semiconductor.md), [`vacuum.deposition-systems`](../vacuum/deposition-systems.md), [`vacuum.pumps`](../vacuum/pumps.md)
> **Outputs**: metal_thin_films, barrier_layers, adhesion_layers
> **Timeline**: Years 45-65
> **Critical**: No

Physical Vapor Deposition (PVD): sputtering (DC, RF, magnetron) and evaporation (thermal, electron-beam) of metal and dielectric thin films in high vacuum. Chamber pressure ~10⁻³ Torr (sputtering with Ar) or 10⁻⁶–10⁻⁸ Torr (evaporation). Deposits Al, Ti, Cu, Ta, W, and other target materials as conductive, barrier, or adhesion layers for IC metallization. Film thickness monitored by quartz crystal microbalance (QCM).
