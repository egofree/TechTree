# Coturnix Quail

> **Node ID**: `animals.poultry.coturnix`
> **Domain**: [Animals](./index.md)
> **Dependencies**: None
> **Enables**: [`animals.poultry`](../animals/poultry.md)
> **Outputs**: quail_eggs, quail_meat, manure
> **Timeline**: Years 0-3
> **Critical**: No

Japanese quail (Coturnix japonica) raised for eggs and meat. Sexual maturity at 6 weeks, producing 300+ eggs per year at 10-12g each. The smallest and fastest-reproducing poultry species, requiring minimal space (25x25 cm cage per bird) and feed (20-25g/day).
