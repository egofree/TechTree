# Transportation & Logistics

Capabilities in this domain:

- [Telegraph Communication](telegraph.md) — Electrical telegraph systems using Morse code over wire for long-distance communication and railway block signaling.

- [Railways](railways.md) — Steam locomotive railways, track construction, signaling, and freight/passenger operations.

- [Diesel-Electric Locomotives](diesel-locomotives.md) — Diesel-electric powertrains, road-switcher design, and dynamic braking for main-line freight and passenger locomotives.

- [Electric Railways](electric-railways.md) — Overhead catenary and third-rail electrification, traction motors, and regenerative braking.

- [High-Speed Rail](high-speed-rail.md) — Wheel-on-rail and magnetic-levitation passenger rail at 200-350+ km/h, integrating aerodynamic trainsets, precision-aligned track, and high-power electric traction.

- [Road & Bridge Construction](roads.md) — Road construction from dirt tracks to paved highways, bridge building, and route planning.

- [Water Transport](shipping.md) — Water transport: boats, barges, canals, and maritime shipping infrastructure.

- [Road Construction Equipment](road-construction-equipment.md) — Graders, rollers, pavers, and excavating equipment for road and highway construction.

- [ICE Powertrains](ice-powertrains.md) — Internal combustion engine powertrains: transmissions (manual and automatic), drivetrain layouts (FWD/RWD/AWD), differentials, and fuel/exhaust systems including emissions aftertreatment.

- [Motor Vehicle Fundamentals](motor-vehicles.md) — Vehicle chassis, body, suspension, steering, and brake systems from motor carriages to automobiles, trucks, and buses.

- [Electric Vehicles](electric-vehicles.md) — Battery electric, hybrid, and fuel cell vehicles: traction motors, battery packs, charging systems, and regenerative braking.

- [Tire Manufacturing](tire-manufacturing.md) — Pneumatic and solid tire manufacturing: rubber compounding, calendered cord fabric, green-tire building, and press curing (vulcanization).

- [Motorcycles & Scooters](motorcycles.md) — Two-wheeled vehicles: frame design, suspension geometry, and ICE/electric powertrain integration from mopeds to sport bikes.
