# Ducks

> **Node ID**: `animals.poultry.ducks`
> **Domain**: [Animals](./index.md)
> **Dependencies**: [`animals.poultry`](./poultry.md)
> **Outputs**: duck_eggs, duck_meat, feathers, manure
> **Timeline**: Years 0-5
> **Critical**: No

Domesticated ducks (Anas platyrhynchos domesticus, Cairina moschata) raised for eggs, meat, and pest control. Khaki Campbell ducks produce 250-340 eggs per year, exceeding most chicken breeds. Ducks are superior foragers of slugs, snails, and aquatic plants, and are more disease-resistant than chickens.
