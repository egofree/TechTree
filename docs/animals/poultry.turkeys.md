# Turkeys

> **Node ID**: `animals.poultry.turkeys`
> **Domain**: [Animals](./index.md)
> **Dependencies**: [`animals.poultry`](./poultry.md)
> **Outputs**: turkey_meat, feathers, manure
> **Timeline**: Years 1-10+
> **Critical**: No

Domesticated turkeys (Meleagris gallopavo) raised for meat. Toms reach 8-15 kg, hens 4-8 kg, with market weight at 14-20 weeks. Turkeys require more space than chickens (20+ m² per bird for range) and are susceptible to blackhead disease, requiring separation from chickens.
