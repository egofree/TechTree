# Float Glass Process

> **Node ID**: `glass.basic.float-glass`
> **Domain**: [Glass](./index.md)
> **Dependencies**: [`energy.electric-furnaces`](../energy/electric-furnaces.md),
> [`gas-handling.basic`](../gas-handling/basic.md),
> [`metals.non-ferrous`](../metals/non-ferrous.md)
> **Outputs**: float_glass, flat_glass_ribbon
> **Timeline**: Years 30-50
> **Critical**: No

Pilkington float glass process: molten glass poured onto a bath of molten tin at ~1000°C in a controlled nitrogen/hydrogen atmosphere, producing perfectly flat, parallel-surface glass ribbon without grinding or polishing. Accounts for >90% of worldwide flat glass production for windows, mirrors, and architectural glazing.
