Domesticated fowl (Gallus gallus domesticus) raised for eggs and meat. The most widely kept poultry species, producing 200-300 eggs per year for modern layers on just 100-150g feed/day. Dual-purpose breeds provide both eggs and meat, reaching slaughter weight at 12-16 weeks on range.
