# Czochralski Crystal Pulling

> **Node ID**: `silicon.crystal-growth.cz-pulling`
> **Domain**: [Silicon](./index.md)
> **Dependencies**: None
> **Enables**: [`measurement.temperature-pressure`](../measurement/temperature-pressure.md), [`silicon.crystal-growth`](../silicon/crystal-growth.md)
> **Outputs**: single_crystal_ingots
> **Timeline**: Years 30-50
> **Critical**: No

Pulling single-crystal silicon ingots from molten polysilicon using a seed crystal in a quartz crucible at ~1415°C. Requires precision leadscrew (±0.5% speed stability), argon atmosphere, and temperature control ±0.5°C. Dash process eliminates dislocations via thin neck growth.
