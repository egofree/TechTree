# Optical Coatings

> **Node ID**: `optics.inspection.optical-coatings`
> **Domain**: [Optics](./index.md)
> **Dependencies**: None
> **Enables**: [`gas-handling.vacuum`](../gas-handling/vacuum.md), [`optics.inspection`](../optics/inspection.md), [`vacuum.pumps`](../vacuum/pumps.md)
> **Outputs**: ar_coatings, mirror_coatings, optical_filters
> **Timeline**: Years 35-50+
> **Critical**: No

Thin-film deposition for optical enhancement: MgF₂ anti-reflection coating (quarter-wave, ~100 nm, thermal evaporation at 10⁻⁴ Pa) and aluminum mirror coating (vacuum evaporation, ~100 nm, 88-92% reflectance). Requires vacuum deposition chamber, tungsten/tantalum boats, thickness monitoring. Enables multi-element lens systems and protected mirrors.
