# Pumped Hydroelectric Storage

> **Node ID**: `energy.storage.pumped-hydro`
> **Domain**: [Energy](./index.md)
> **Dependencies**: [`energy.electricity`](./electricity.md), [`energy.gravity`](./gravity.md)
> **Outputs**: grid_energy_storage, peak_power
> **Timeline**: Years 25-50
> **Critical**: No

Reversible pump-turbine systems storing energy by pumping water uphill to a reservoir, recovering 70-85% via turbine generation on release. The most mature grid-scale energy storage technology, essential for matching intermittent supply with demand.
